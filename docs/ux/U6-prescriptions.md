# U6 · Prescriptions

The coach's suggestions, rendered with their homework showing. v1 is layer-1 (deterministic) only — 09 §4's composer is tabled, and nothing here waits on it. **Every sentence on these surfaces is a filled template over computed numbers** ("dropped 7ths at 3× your usual · 14 times across 9 reads" is arithmetic, not generation) — v1 never promises prose it can't compute; nuance arrives with layer 2 or not at all.

**Mockup:** [mockups/U6-prescriptions.html](mockups/U6-prescriptions.html)

## The shelf (in the Practice picker's prescription section, U1 — never on the home page)

Up to three cards (09's cap). Each card: a plain-language title ("Sevenths in flat keys") · the **evidence sentence** in plain words ("dropped 7ths at 3× your usual · 14 times across 9 reads") · a target preview (drill chips or a generation-bias chip) · **Accept** / **Decline**. Accepted → it becomes a runnable block chip (and boosts serving, 04 §6). Declined → gone for two weeks unless its evidence worsens; expired → gone quietly (09 §2).

## "Why am I drilling this" (the evidence view)

A first-class screen, not a tooltip (09 §3). From any prescribed drill (info affordance in U2's status strip) or from the shelf card: the diagnosis, its rule, the evidence window (tag counts, baseline comparison, the reads/reps involved — each tappable to its logged attempt), and the prescription's spec. Explainability is the trust mechanism; nothing the coach does is unexplained.

## Outcomes, surfaced

- **Paid off:** a retirement card in the bout summary and History ("that dotted-rhythm drill paid off — dotted rhythms down 60%"), echoed in the attribution feed.
- **Still there:** an unchanged/worsened outcome surfaces as a plain observation ("still seeing 6/8 barline hesitations — the drills haven't moved it yet"). Pointing out the pattern **is** the intervention (09 §3); no auto-escalation, no nagging repeat.

## Tabled (renders nothing in v1)

Hypothesis-tag chips ("watching your 6/8 barlines") and composer-written notes arrive with layer 2. The layout reserves no dead space for them — they slot into the shelf and coach note when they exist.
