# U4 · Summary, Ratings & History

Where practice becomes a story: the post-bout summary, the Ratings tab (leagues), and History.

**Mockup:** [mockups/U4-summary-history.html](mockups/U4-summary-history.html)

## Bout summary (a sheet, when a bout closes — 08 §6)

Report, never scold:

- **What strengthened:** count + tappable drill chips ("14 drills strengthened").
- **Reads:** taken, with any axis moves as before→after chips (`rhythm C- → C+`).
- **Notable tags:** at most three, phrased as observations ("hesitations clustered at barlines").
- Streak tick. A retired-with-credit prescription shows its "paid off" card here.
- Dismiss → Practice. Summaries archive to History; there is no "incomplete bout" state.

## Ratings tab

- **Composite hero card:** league + division + number ("**Gold II** · 840") with the **spread strip** beneath — the weak domain plainly visible (07's rule: never hidden).
- **The ratings radar, BBA-style:** the domains on a radar chart whose concentric rings are **league bands in their metals** (bronze innermost → master at the rim); **the value polygon wears the composite's league color** — the composite is the derived mean of the four (07 §3), so it colors the shape rather than getting a fake fifth vertex that would always sit at the average; **each vertex dot wears its own domain's league**. Four domains draw a diamond today; a real fifth domain (the pinned audio/listening expansion) would make it a true pentagon. **Tapping a vertex opens the domain detail.**
- **Domain detail** (per vertex): the league chip in its metal, and a **real trend chart, never a bare sparkline** — labeled axes (rating × months), **league and division boundaries demarcated as dashed lines in their metals**, zone labels at the right, and **transition points marked on the line** ("reached Gold · Jun"). Below it: **what feeds this domain** (its families and reads) and recent attribution lines — every rating change traceable to its cause.
- **Attribution feed:** every rating change as a plain sentence with its cause ("Keys & Chords +12 — dom7 gates passed in flats"). This is 07 §6 rendered.
- **Benchmark invitation:** a card, monthly (or after 30+ days away) — "Ready to measure? ~15 minutes." Dismissable; returns next month; never a badge, never a count.
- **Retention view:** the interleaving-bargain chart — rating trends over weeks against practice mix — the "feels worse, works better" evidence (07's thesis-measurement duty).
- League movement: one tasteful celebration moment on promotion (a shimmer in the league's own metal on the card — colors mean things); demotions state themselves in a plain sentence in the feed, uncelebrated and unhidden.

## History

- **Bouts:** reverse-chronological summary cards.
- **Benchmarks:** past results with per-segment detail and excerpt replays.
- **Coach notes:** the weekly three-bullet cards (09 §5) — what moved · what's weak · what's prescribed/paid off.
