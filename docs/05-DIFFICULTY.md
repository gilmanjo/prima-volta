# 05 — Difficulty

One ladder orders everything: passage generation (06), rating anchors (07), and the pre-paving of drill admission (02). This doc is the authoritative ladder, the admission rules, and the staircase that moves a user along it.

## 1. The ladder (authoritative)

Nine ranks, **F → E → D → C → B → A → S → SS → SSS**, cumulative — spliced from the ABRSM sight-reading progression (F–D ≈ pre-Grade 1; SSS ≈ Grade 5–6 sight-reading), RCM's "3 below exam level" rule, and SRF's parameter surface. Each cell names what *enters the pool*; everything earlier stays in it. The grade anchors keep the repertoire bridge honest — "reading at B" still tells you which real pieces are in reach.

| Rank | Hands / texture | Pitch | Rhythm / meter | Key axis | Length / other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | One hand, 5-finger position | Steps, repeats | basic (♩ 𝅗𝅥 𝅝) · 4/4 | C | 4 bars · ♩=60 · rests at repositions |
| E | Each hand alone | + 3rds | + 3/4 · basic completes (dotted-half, ties) | + G, F | Landmark starts · fragments to 3rds |
| D | HT: one hand sustains | + 4ths/5ths | + 2/4 · eighths | + Am | f / p |
| C | HT parallel rhythm | Leaps to 5th · to 2 ledger lines | dotted · upbeats | + D · single accidentals | 8 bars · slur/staccato · scale fragments, 4th–6th shapes |
| B | Independent rhythms | 2-note blocked intervals | + 6/8, cut time · sixteenths (pairs) | 2♯/2♭ + Dm, Em | Shift by step · triad shapes |
| A | Melody + interval/broken-chord accomp. · voice crossing | 6ths, octave leap | + 3/8 · syncopation (simple) | 3♯/♭ + harmonic minors | Shift by leap · mp/mf · broken-chord figures |
| S | Root-position triads | Spans to octave | sixteenths (full patterns) · triplets · rests-everywhere | 4♯/♭ · secondary chords | 12 bars · simple pedal · cadence shapes |
| SS | Inversions; 2 voices/hand | Leaps > octave · deep ledger (3+) | + 9/8, 12/8 · syncopation (cross-bar) | 5♯/♭ + chromatic neighbors | Grace notes |
| SSS | 7th-chord streams; multi-voice per hand | Full keyboard | meter changes · sixteenths (32nds) · triplets (2-vs-3) | All keys + modulation | 16–20+ bars · excerpt style, inner dynamics |

**Cells speak the feeder vocabulary** (§2 reads admissions off these rows, so the content consumers need lives here): the Rhythm/meter column names **F7's nested vocab sets** (basic → eighths → dotted → sixteenths → syncopation → triplets → rests-everywhere; each set's exact members — ties, dotted-half, 32nds, 2-vs-3 — are that set's data entry in F7); the fragment/shape entries in Length are **F8's pattern families**; the ledger entries in Pitch are **F2's register bands**. Column → axis: Hands/texture → texture · Pitch → range · Rhythm/meter → rhythm · Key → keys · Length: **bar counts and fragment entries → span**, shift entries → range, dynamics/articulation → expression (06 §8 — engraved reading content, ungraded). The **rests-at-repositions** requirement (F–D) is a referee constant that relaxes as the range rank climbs.

**Subranks** `--` `-` `+` `++` `+++` within each rank nudge the continuous knobs — tempo, bars, note density (inter-onset interval), hand-displacement rate, accidental count — the feature set the difficulty-aware-generation literature validates. Promotion walks subranks; ranks change what exists, subranks change how much of it per minute. **Each knob belongs to exactly one axis**: keys → accidental load · rhythm → tempo & note density · texture → voice independence · range → hand-displacement rate · span → bars and lookahead demand (the Length column's bar counts and fragment entries are its row). An axis's subrank turns only its own dials.

A position is written rank-then-subrank — `B+`, `C--` (just entered C), `C+++` (knocking on B's door) — and stored as ordinals (rank 0–8, subrank 0–4). Positions form **one fully ordered sequence** — `F--` … `F+++` → `E--` … → `SSS+++` — and the staircase steps along it one subrank at a time in either direction, crossing rank boundaries both ways: `B+++` promotes to `A--`, and `A--` demotes to `B+++`. `SSS+++` has no promotion; `F--` has no demotion. Ranks are letters; family **tiers** (T-numbers) are an unrelated concept of the catalog (00 glossary).

**The key axis staggering** (shared by generation and admission) is the ladder's own key column: C → +G, F → +Am → +D and single accidentals → 2♯/2♭ +Dm, Em → 3♯/♭ +harmonic minors → 4♯/♭ (secondary chords) → 5♯/♭ +chromatic neighbors → all keys +modulation. Minor *content* rides drill-scope defaults (02 §5) — the ladder defines order, scope defines whether.

## 2. Admission — no global gate, no unlock table

There is no scalar user level (the staircase is per-axis; nothing in the design even produces one), so nothing global gates atom admission. Availability is decided two local ways:

- **Family-local (the main path):** each family's own admission ladder — its tier order × the key wave (× a quality order where relevant, e.g. F4) — is seed data (02); frontier-on-pull walks it as the user practices that family, and pace is self-limiting: the next wave admits as the previous wave's atoms hold strength, so nobody outruns their own evidence. Every family's first rung is available from day one; scope decides what's visible.
- **Reading-coupled (pre-paving):** when an engine-C axis **enters** a rank, the drill vocabulary that the *next* rank's passages will demand admits in its feeder families (the rhythm axis entering C admits F7's B-rank vocabulary, and so on — transitively, so a placement that seeds an axis high admits its whole feeder swath at once). Passages therefore never demand cold content — admission makes the feeder drills available a rank early, and **the promotion itself waits on that content being demonstrated** (§3's readiness gate): the drills-one-step-ahead stagger, expressed per-axis and event-driven. The prescriber (09) may also surface feeder drills early as an axis nears promotion.

  **The feeder map** is small, and the ladder is its own vocabulary table. *Which families feed which axis* is fixed data — **keys** ← F1 signatures, F2 key-signature contexts, and the key-wave step in F4/F5/F6 · **rhythm** ← F7 (meter × vocab classes) · **texture** ← F4 hands/forms tiers and F8's triadShape/brokenChord families · **range** ← F9 spans and F2 ledger bands · **span** ← F8 fragment patterns (F11 is engine C itself, not a feeder). *What admits* is read off the ladder: entering rank X admits, in that axis's feeder families, the atoms/classes whose content the ladder's X+1 row names. One table, single-sourced — no second vocabulary list to drift.

Either source admits; they never wait on each other — a player who only drills chords advances F4 by the first rule alone. Gate tiers are untouched by all of this: gates advance per-card on that card's own performance (02); admission only orders what is *new*.

## 3. The staircase (engine C's per-axis profile)

Five axes, each holding a rank·subrank position independently: **keys · rhythm · texture · range · reading-span**. Generation samples each axis at its own position (a player can be keys-A but rhythm-C).

**Figures:** [the band controller at work](figures/figures.html#band-controller) · [three climbers](figures/figures.html#climbers) · [the oscillation sawtooth](figures/figures.html#sawtooth)

- **Axis scoring is tag-filtered.** A read yields one pitch score, but its error events carry cause tags (03 §5), so each pitch axis scores the read on the errors *attributable to it*: keys ← `accidental` and `key`/`key-region:*` tags · range ← `leap>5th` and `shift:*` tags plus `wrongOctave` events · texture ← `texture:*` and `chord-tone:*` tags (multi-voice context = `texture:multi-voice`; hand attribution rides the event's `hand` field). Untagged pitch errors count against all three. **Rhythm** scores from the rhythm axis of performance reads and from F7 (an F7 rep of a class mapped to the axis's current rank enters at its onsets-in-window percentage); **continuity** never feeds the staircase — it is scored and shown per read (03 §1.2) and enters only the benchmark's sanctioned composite (07 §4), never an ordinary rating. Rehearsal and performance reads both feed the pitch-scored axes; locked-pulse skills advance only on locked-pulse evidence (resolves 03's old question).
- **An entry is a per-read percentage of notes**, never a pass/fail of reads: the share of that read's expected notes that were clean by the axis's own measure — pitch axes count only errors carrying their tags; rhythm counts onsets inside the timing window (03 §4); span counts notes correct past the vanish point. "Mean ≥ 95" therefore means "~19 in 20 notes clean on this axis's terms across recent reads here."
- **Bands — one statistic, three behaviors.** Each axis keeps a rolling window of its last **10** at-position entries (a move clears it; no band action before 5 entries — the 3-consecutive-<70 collapse bail is exempt and may fire from the third entry):

  | rolling mean | behavior |
  |---|---|
  | ≥ 95 | **promote** a subrank |
  | 85–95 | **hold** — the working band, where practice pays |
  | < 85 | **demote** a subrank (and 3 consecutive entries <70 demotes immediately — the collapse bail) |

- **Rank promotions additionally wait on readiness** (subrank promotions turn knobs only and never wait): an axis with a promotion-worthy mean at X+++ steps into the next rank only when the entering rank's feeder content is **ready** — its feeder atoms/classes are **graduated** (real practiced evidence, 04 §2), or that content was **read cleanly in placement or benchmark excerpts** (a successful test read *is* demonstration). An earned-but-held promotion costs nothing and is surfaced by the prescriber's pre-paving nudge (09 rule 15 — "ready to climb; these drills are the gate"); admission (§2) makes readiness cheap to earn, and the ceiling-hunting benchmark is exempt by nature (07 §1). The generator never checks readiness — the served axis position already encodes it.
  **Hysteresis:** a 3-entry cooldown after any move suppresses both moves and accumulation. **Anti-flapping invariant:** one subrank's difficulty step must cost less clean-rate than the hold band is wide (~3–5 points per subrank against the 10-point band) — calibration (§5) enforces it, so a move lands *inside* the working band, never across it.
- **Why these numbers are stricter than SRS apps'.** The suite has three different "error rates," and only one tolerates errors: FSRS's desired retention (~0.85–0.9, 04) is a *scheduling-efficiency* knob — a lapsed memory costs a relearn, nothing more; engine-A/B grading is *accuracy-absolute per rep* (any error → Again, 03 §6 — the unit is one chord, so perfection is well-defined and difficulty lives entirely in the clock); these bands govern *what execution gets rehearsed* on novel passages. A motor error is not a cheap training label — it is a rep of the wrong program. Errorless/implicit motor-learning research (Masters & Maxwell's programme) finds low-error acquisition builds skill that survives pressure and dual-task load, while the ~85%-optimal training results (Wilson et al. 2019) come from perceptual *classification* and don't transfer — and slow-practice orthodoxy has said the same thing for a century. So: **perfect practice is the target; 95 concedes only grader false-negatives** (03 §1.5's trust principle), never human slips. **Tempo is the dial** — rehearsal reads are self-paced, so accuracy stays the constant while speed absorbs the difficulty.
- **Reading-span has its own measure.** Its entries are F11 accuracy-past-the-vanish-point (a percentage; band rules as above) and F8 span latency mapped to per-subrank **latency bands** (seed data); performance reads at length never touch it — length *demands* what span has measured, it doesn't measure span. **Pre-F11 policy:** until F11 ships, span moves on F8 bands alone. Both directions work from day one — the same evidence that promotes span can demote it.
- **Stretch reads** (offered roughly 1 in 5 reads, one subrank up on one named axis): a stretch that itself clears the promote threshold joins that axis's promotion mean at **half weight**; anything less counts nowhere — never toward demotion, never dragging the mean. A stretch can help, never hurt. Full weight as diagnosis evidence either way; promotion is earned at the served position.
- **Placement:** first-run test of **~6 excerpts plus two flash probes, ~2 minutes** (07's benchmark blueprint at reduced length; placement reads use a one-glance preview, not the full ritual — that's how it fits in two minutes). Excerpts seed keys/rhythm/texture/range; the flash probes seed span, keeping its evidence pure from day one. Through §2's coupling, seeding admits each axis's feeder swath, so a well-placed player's drill pool matches their reading immediately. The staircase converges from there; monthly benchmarks re-anchor.

## 4. Difficulty function (the generator's referee)

A pure function `difficulty(score) → per-axis vector` computed from the finished exercise: pitch range and leap census, IOI density, hand-displacement rate, accidental load, texture class, bars. Generation is accepted only if the score conforms per axis — the generator proposes, the function verifies (and it is the unit-testable statement of what each rank means). Conformance is **categorical-exact** for vocabulary content (only in-pool keys, meters, vocab items, figure and leap classes may appear at all) and **tolerance-banded** for the continuous knobs (density, displacement rate, accidental count, bars); texture is classified **from the notation surface**, never from generation metadata (06 §4).

## 5. Calibration

Predicted vs observed: every read logs its requested vector and its axis scores; a weekly job fits observed error rates per knob. If a knob's realized difficulty drifts from its intended step (e.g., 6/8 spikes error rates two subranks' worth), the knob's weight is retuned — **the ladder's shape is fixed; the knobs' calibration is data**. Monthly benchmarks (07) anchor absolute placement; the SASR-style excerpt bank doubles as the calibration set.

The staircase constants — band edges 95/85, collapse floor 70, the 10-entry window (act at 5), the 3-entry cooldown — are **v0 values**: decidable only empirically. A spike-stage simulation (13's harness in its spike life: a synthetic reader with per-axis learning curves run against the staircase) sanity-checks the dynamics before code, with named scenarios: the **80% overreacher** (must step down), the **90% worker** (must hold and improve — the working band is where practice pays, never a basement), the **flapper** (an adjacent-subrank clean-rate gap wider than the hold band must be impossible after knob calibration), and the **crawler** (climbs content difficulty at extreme slowness — slow-but-clean is legitimate slow practice, but verify the climb stays healthy; a per-rank tempo floor is the lever if it doesn't). The spike's **step-size probe** measures the realized oscillation both ways, and it dissolved the apparent tension between 03 §1.4's ~87–97 and the 3–5-point step: **two statistics of one behavior** — per-read clean spans ~91–99 in the v0 sim (the ~87–97 experience, with the low tail depending on player noise) while the rolling mean the bands act on rides ~94–95. The probe stays as a regression check; live logs confirm the per-read span. Live review logs retune the constants after.

## 6. Worked examples

A profile mid-journey: **keys A-- · rhythm C- · texture C-- · range B+ · span D++** — flats ahead of syncopation, as is normal for an adult learner.

**One rehearsal read, end to end.** The user opens a Reading block; engine C builds the request by sampling each axis at its own position: key from A's 3♯/♭ + harmonic-minor pool (E♭ major today), rhythm from C's vocabulary (4/4, dotted ♩+♪, an upbeat — no 6/8, no 16ths yet), texture HT-parallel, leaps capped at a 5th with step-shifts only, 6 bars at ♩=76. The generator proposes; `difficulty(score)` measures the actual engraving (leap census, IOI density, accidental load) — a stray 6th leap bounces it for regeneration. Preview ritual (one question: "largest leap?"), count-in, one pass. Scores: **Pitch 86 · Rhythm 74 · Continuity 91**, both pitch errors tagged `accidental`. Feeding: tag-filtering routes them — the keys window logs an 86 while texture and range log clean 100s (no tag of theirs fired); the 74 never touches the rhythm axis (rehearsal read); the 91 is shown on the post-read screen and goes no further — continuity enters only the benchmark composite (07 §4).

**Promotion, with atoms pre-paving.** The rhythm axis moves only on performance reads and F7. Its window's last five entries: performance-read rhythm scores 96, 94, 97, plus two clean F7 dotted-vocab reps at gate tempo entering at their onsets-in-window percentages, 95 and 98 → rolling mean 96 ≥ 95 → rhythm C- → C+ (a notch denser and faster; keys untouched). At C+++ the *rank* turns: promotion into B brings 6/8 and 16th pairs into passages — vocabulary that admitted to F7 drills back when the rhythm axis entered C, **and the promotion itself waited on those classes being graduated** (§3's readiness gate), so no passage ever demands cold content.

**Demotion, hysteresis, and what doesn't happen.** A tired evening: three performance reads score rhythm 55/48/58 → three consecutive entries under 70, the collapse bail → rhythm steps C+ → C-, then the 3-entry cooldown blocks further movement. The keys axis (pitch 88/85/90 that same evening) is untouched; no atom's FSRS state is touched (passages aren't cards); the continuity dip shows on the rating, not the staircase.

**Stretch read.** Offered ~1 in 5: content one subrank up on one named axis. A stretch 96 joins that axis's **promotion mean at half weight**; a stretch 88 counts nowhere — a stretch can help, never hurt. Full diagnosis weight either way; promotion is earned at the served position.

**F11 and the span axis.** Span D++ serves vanish-on-onset reads at 6 bars with position shifts telegraphed early; its entries are accuracy-past-the-vanish and F8 latency bands — the same evidence moves it in both directions, and performance reads at length never touch it (until F11 ships, F8 bands alone carry it). Crossing into C lengthens passages to 8 bars and telegraphs shifts later — F10 inherits the lookahead demand F11 measured.
