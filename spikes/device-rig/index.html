1 · Latency & jitter — the calibration ritual (03 §3)

Four count-in clicks (low tone) to entrain, then eight scored clicks (high tone) — press any key exactly on each high click. Anticipation outliers are dropped; the median offset becomes the profile's latency correction, the spread its jitter.

2 · Velocity floor

First brush keys as lightly as you can (accidental-touch territory, ~10 touches), then click "now play normally" and play ~10 intended soft notes. The gap between the two clusters is the floor.

3 · Hesitation baseline

Play something easy and even, self-paced (a C-major scale up and down a few times). Reports the IOI distribution and how many onsets would flag at 1.5× / 2× / 2.5× the local median — grounding 03's "IOI > 2× local median" proposal.
